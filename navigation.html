Safety: This prototype uses GPS and mapped walking routes. GPS may drift, mapped paths may be incomplete, and speech may lag. It cannot reliably detect vehicles, stairs, holes, or immediate hazards. Do not use it as your only aid near roads or crossings. Keep using your cane, guide dog, or trusted assistance.

Spoken language

Voice recognition depends on browser and language support. If unavailable, type the destination. Location requires HTTPS and permission.

Walking directions use a public OpenStreetMap-based demo routing service. Step counts are rough estimates from distance (assumed stride 0.7 m), not actual counted steps. Test with a trusted guide before any real-world use.
